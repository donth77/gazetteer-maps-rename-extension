Gazetteer

Control what Google Maps calls things.

Gazetteer renames places in the sidebar, in search results, in the tab title, and on the map itself. If a label was already on screen before you saved a change, it updates the next time the map redraws. Pan, zoom, or reload the tab to see it right away.

Backup

Export saves your renames to a file, for backup or for sharing. Import replaces everything with the contents of a file.
